01 · The idea

What is a Whitelabel?

  • We run alternative price-comparison platforms, operated by idealo through its subsidiary Panther Holding.
  • We market idealo's and the merchants' inventory under other brands.
  • We operate in the same countries as idealo — multiple brands across Germany, Spain, France, the UK, Italy and Austria.
Same underlying marketplace, presented under different brands and domains. We're part of idealo, not a competitor to it.
02 · The mental model

The core difference vs. idealo

idealo
Product → Offers

The product is the central entity — content, SEO and marketing build on it. A user searches "iPhone 16" and lands on a product page.

Whitelabels
Query → Offers

Users traditionally search generic terms — e.g. "cheap smartphones" — and land on a query page. E.g. auspreiser.de → "Smartphones günstig".

→ Our strategy is increasingly moving toward stronger product orientation.
The single most important thing to remember. idealo is product-first; the whitelabels were query-first — and that line is now blurring.
03 · The rationale

Why do we do this?

More domains = more reach & more market share.

  • Occupy additional positions in classic Google search — and displace competitors.
  • Generic keywords are often cheaper than heavily-contested product keywords.
  • Lower volume each, but a huge variety of queries → comparable traffic overall, sometimes at lower acquisition cost.
A different business model: many cheap, low-volume generic queries add up to serious traffic — a volume game, not fighting over the same expensive keywords.
04 · Where it fits

A complement to idealo

Not every category works well in a strict product structure — especially where products…

  • searched less by brand / specific product

Built to cover the long tail — now shifting from query-based toward a more product-oriented model.
